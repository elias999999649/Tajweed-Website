# Audio and font provenance — 27 October 2026

The new recording is Al-Fatihah, recited by Ali al-Hudhaify in Hafs ʿan ʿĀṣim, published by the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex. Its official playlist contains 114 surah recordings. The file was downloaded unchanged from the first playlist entry and is served locally. The publisher identifies the reciter and reading on its [recording page](https://qurancomplex.gov.sa/quran-audio-hafs-huthify/); its [usage policy](https://qurancomplex.gov.sa/quran-audios/) permits free general use of the listed digital recordings in websites and applications. This permission applies to those recordings; it is not a license for arbitrary Quran.com or EveryAyah audio. No endorsement of this website is implied. No altered-speed recording or word timestamps are supplied.

Original file: `https://download.qurancomplex.gov.sa/new-sounds/huthify-01/hafs/sura/10-001D00-A01.mp3`. Local file: `public/audio/al-fatihah-ali-al-hudhaify.mp3` (505,984 bytes). Source availability and file hash are recorded in `source-health-2026-10-27.json`.

Google Sans Flex is retained from the existing project, including Turkish glyphs. Amiri Regular and Bold were fetched from the official Google Fonts repository and converted losslessly from TTF to WOFF2 with fontTools. Font licenses ship in `public/licenses/`. Builds and runtime font loading no longer require a connection to Google. The source font families and the homepage verse remain intact.

Microphone access is requested only after the learner starts recording in the listening studio. A recording lasts at most two minutes, remains in memory, and is removed on page exit unless downloaded. It is never uploaded, analysed, or used to issue a pronunciation score. The microphone permission policy now allows this origin; camera and geolocation remain disabled.

Public source checks verify provenance, technical availability, and documented permission. They do not constitute a teacher's approval of lesson wording, rule annotations, translations, or a learner's recitation. Those approval records remain empty rather than naming a source author or reciter as this site's reviewer.
